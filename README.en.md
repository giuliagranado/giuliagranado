## 🌟 Featured Projects

### 🗺️ [Crime Map (Mapa do Crime)](https://github.com/giuliagranado/Mapa-do-Crime_PI-1)

Academic research on crime in Baixada Santista between 2017 and 2024. An ETL pipeline turns raw SSP-SP data into interactive Power BI dashboards.

- Two views: one by **number of incidents** and another by **rate per 100,000 inhabitants**, which makes cities of different sizes comparable.
- Filters by crime type, city, month and year, plus a map of the region.

**Tech:** Python (Pandas, NumPy), SQL, Excel, Power BI

### 🚢 [PortoHack-2026 (DataWave)](https://github.com/giuliagranado/PortoHack-2026)

Solution for Porto Hack Santos 2026. It predicts a cargo's retention risk and recommends the best destination: quayside or off-dock terminal. It also audits the DUIMP product catalog before customs clearance.

- Monte Carlo risk simulation with **5,000 iterations**, estimating cargo dwell time and the probability of exceeding free time.
- Demurrage and detention costs, plus the **break-even day** between quayside and off-dock storage.
- Customs broker spreadsheet parsing (CSV, TSV, JSON) and NCM attribute checks by a Logcomex agent, via MCP.
- **46 automated tests** covering the calculation engine, schemas and API.

**Tech:** Python, FastAPI, Pydantic, Pytest, HTML/JS, MCP
